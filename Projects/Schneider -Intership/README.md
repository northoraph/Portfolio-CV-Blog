# Internship — Schneider Electric SE, Grenoble (Digital Power R&D)

- Praxissemester (mandatory internship), Apr–Aug 2024, "Electropole" site, Grenoble
- Report: `Internship Report rev_A.docx`; final presentation: `Praxissemester bei Schneider Electric SE.pptx`

## Essence
Work in the R&D department of Schneider Electric's Digital Power line of business on **Project Bluebird**, a new wireless power-measurement sensor (PowerTag family) built around Rogowski coils and capacitive voltage measurement, integrated in the EcoStruxure ecosystem (Panel Server, circuit breakers).

Main contributions:
- **Lab assistance**: uncertainty testing of sensors against IEC 61557-12 (measurement performance classes for active power/energy)
- **Modeling a switch algorithm**: MATLAB/Simulink/Stateflow model of the sensor's MCU operating modes (acquisition radio on/off, high/low accuracy, sleep, save data) to manage the trade-off between measurement accuracy and energy consumption
