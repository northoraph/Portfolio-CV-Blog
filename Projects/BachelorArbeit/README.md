# Bachelor Thesis — Impact of Photovoltaic Expansion on Urban Power Grids (Munich)

**Auswirkungen des Photovoltaik-Ausbaus auf städtische Stromnetze: Untersuchung am Beispiel Münchens**

- B.Eng. thesis, TH Augsburg, in cooperation with SWM Infrastruktur GmbH & Co. KG (Stadtwerke München)
- Supervisor: Prof. Dr.-Ing. Michael Finkel; company supervisor: Dominik Storch
- Submitted: May 2024

## Essence
Investigates how the ongoing expansion of rooftop photovoltaics affects Munich's urban distribution grid. Covers PV fundamentals (cells, modules, cell technologies, solar irradiation models, grid integration, IEC performance metrics), then models the power generation of PV systems (e.g. a 1 kWp reference plant simulated over an average year from 2005–2021 irradiation data for Munich, tilt 30°, south-facing) and evaluates the resulting load flows and hosting capacity of the city's low/medium-voltage networks.
