# High School Exchange in Canada

During my time at the Gymnasium, I spent a high school exchange in Canada, attending the **Centennial Collegiate Vocational Institute (CCVI)** in Guelph, Ontario.

Living with a host family and going to a Canadian school day-to-day was a very different experience from school life in Germany: choosing my own course schedule, joining school sports teams and clubs, and being immersed in English around the clock. It was my first longer stay abroad on my own, and it shaped both my English and my confidence in navigating a new environment — something that later made moving to France for my studies feel much more natural.

Guelph itself, about an hour west of Toronto, gave me a great mix of small-city life and easy access to trips — from Toronto and Niagara Falls to the Canadian outdoors.
