# My Terahertz Project at TU Darmstadt

During the summer term of 2025, I took part in the "Project in Entrepreneurship and Innovation Management" course at TU Darmstadt, working alongside the Institute of Microwave Engineering and Photonics (IMP) on their Terahertz Photonic Vector Network Analyzer (TPVNA).

## The Challenge

With an estimated 10-year time-to-market horizon, the goal wasn't to build the product — it was to validate it. Our team was tasked with running an early market analysis: understanding who would actually want a device like this, what they'd be willing to pay for it, and what features would matter most.

## What We Did

We designed and ran an online survey together with IMP, reached out to engineers, researchers and industry experts via LinkedIn and email, and conducted personal interviews to dig deeper into the survey results. I also authored a one-page project summary about the TPVNA to give potential respondents an accessible introduction to a fairly technical topic.

The findings — on realistic pricing, must-have features, and recommendations for further development — were compiled into a report and presented to the project supervisors and IMP.

## What I Took Away

It was a great reminder that deep-tech products live or die on market validation just as much as on engineering excellence. Coordinating a team, translating a highly technical invention into something a non-specialist could understand, and turning survey data into concrete recommendations was a genuinely rewarding combination of entrepreneurship and engineering.

The project earned me a recommendation letter from Prof. Dr. Carolin Bock, which I'm happy to share below.
