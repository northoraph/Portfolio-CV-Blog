# Growing Up Franco-German

I grew up between two languages and two cultures: German and French. Switching between them was never something I thought about — it was simply how everyday life worked, whether at the dinner table, on family visits or on holidays.

Looking back, that upbringing shaped more than just my languages. It taught me early on that there is rarely only one "normal" way of doing things — from food and humor to how people argue, celebrate and work. Moving between the two worlds made adapting to new environments feel natural long before I ever lived abroad on my own.

It also left very practical traces in my path: sailing courses and the Permis Côtier on the French coast, a high school exchange in Canada that felt like a smaller step thanks to being used to switching contexts, an internship at Schneider Electric in Grenoble, and finally the double degree at École Centrale de Lyon — where studying engineering in French closed the circle in a way.

Today, being bilingual in German and French (plus fluent English) is one of the things I value most, both personally and professionally — especially in engineering teams that work across borders.
