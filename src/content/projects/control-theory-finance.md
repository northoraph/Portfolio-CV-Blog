A personal project exploring what control theory has to say about financial markets.

The starting intuition is that a portfolio under active management is a feedback system: you observe a noisy state, you act on it, your action changes the state, and there is a delay in between. That framing brings familiar questions with it — how much gain before the loop oscillates, what the observer can actually estimate from noisy measurements, and how robust a strategy is to a model that is wrong in the ways models are usually wrong.
